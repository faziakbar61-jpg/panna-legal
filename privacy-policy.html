Privacy Policy

Panna: Street Football Online  ·  package com.asinteractive.panna
Published by A&S Interactive  ·  Last updated 30 July 2026

In short: Panna does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Everything the game remembers is stored only on your own device.

1. Information we collect

None. Panna does not collect or transmit any personal or device information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact detail
  • Your location, at any level of precision
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP-address-based identifiers
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone input, or camera input
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, performance telemetry, or behavioural data

The app requests no runtime permissions. It declares internet access so that future online features can work, but the current version makes no network requests that transmit information about you.

2. Information stored on your device

Panna saves your own settings and preferences locally, using Android's standard app-storage mechanism. This includes:

  • Game settings — audio, graphics quality, control preferences, camera options
  • Your cosmetic choices — kit colours, hairstyle, jersey number, chosen display name
  • Match-format preference and single-player progress

This data never leaves your device. It is not readable by us or by anyone else. Uninstalling Panna deletes all of it permanently.

3. Children's privacy

Panna is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone of any age — because we do not collect information at all. The app is rated for ages 13 and over.

4. Third-party services and SDKs

The current version of Panna contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no social-media SDK. It is distributed through Google Play; Google's own handling of your Play Store activity is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and is outside our control.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. There is no user data to share.

6. Data security

Because Panna stores information only on your own device and transmits none of it, there is no server-side store of personal data that could be breached.

7. Your rights

You can delete all data Panna has stored at any time by uninstalling the app, or by using Android's Settings → Apps → Panna → Storage → Clear data. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase on your behalf. If you would like written confirmation of that, contact us using the address below.

8. Future changes to this policy

Panna is in active development. Planned future versions may add online multiplayer, which would require creating an anonymous player account, and voice or text chat between players. Features like those necessarily involve processing some data.

We will update this policy, and the Data Safety declaration on our Google Play listing, before any release that changes what is collected — not afterwards. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change when that happens.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in Panna, can be sent to A&S Interactive at the developer email address shown on our Google Play listing.
